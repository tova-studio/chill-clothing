Welcome to Chill Clothing Limited

Who are Chill Clothing?

We are proud to be a small New Zealand enterprise offering quality products using the best fabric, threads and superior workmanship. From our base on Gainsborough Street in Christchurch, we can provide and service all of your requirements, from clothing design to the most detailed embroidery.

Using the latest technology, we can digitise and embroider to your individual specifications. Whether it is for your business, a personal project or your corporate image, we know we can add that extra touch to your garment requirements, and we welcome any enquiries.

What we do

What can Chill Clothing make for you?

Embroidery, digitising, clothing and school gear, all handled by one Christchurch team.

Embroidery

From simple detail to the most intricate design, we embroider to your individual specifications using the best threads and the latest technology.

Digitising

We take your design and convert it into a matching image that can be embroidered onto any garment, bringing your image that much more into focus.

Clothing

We can provide and service all of your clothing requirements, from clothing design through to a finished garment made with the best fabric.

School Gear

School gear is one of our specialist ranges. Phone or email us with what you need and we will talk it through with you.
